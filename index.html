INSTALL

Up and running in 2 minutes

It installs from a file, so it's a couple of extra clicks compared to the Chrome Web Store.

  1. Download the zip with the button above.
  2. Make a folder you'll keep, like Documents\Pump HQ Scanner. Right-click the zip, choose Extract All, and extract it there. Don't delete that folder: the extension runs from it.
  3. Open brave://extensions (or chrome://extensions) and turn on Developer mode at the top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick that folder.
  5. Click the puzzle piece and pin Pump HQ Scanner. Open it, paste your Scanner key (Whop → Pump HQ Scanner → Software) and hit Activate. Nothing turns on until you do.
  6. Refresh Axiom's Pulse page. That's it.

Updates: when the popup says a new version is out, download it again, extract it over the same folder, and click the reload arrow on the extensions page.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is it safe?

It's read-only. It never reads your wallet, never signs anything and never trades. The privacy policy lists exactly what it sends and where.

Why do I need Developer mode?

The Scanner installs from a file instead of the Chrome Web Store, and browsers only allow that with Developer mode on. Your browser may remind you about it now and then. Leave it on, or the extension turns off.

How do updates work?

The popup tells you when there's a new version. Download it again, extract it over the same folder, and hit the reload arrow on the extensions page. Your settings and key stay put.

How do I get a Scanner key?

Scanner keys are a monthly subscription on Whop, and everything in the Scanner runs on one. Pump HQ members get the member price, so join Pump HQ first.

Does a GREEN trap check mean it's safe to buy?

No. It means no obvious traps were found. Most pump.fun coins still die, so size small and never trade money you can't lose. Nothing here is financial advice.
