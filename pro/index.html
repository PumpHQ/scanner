QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is it safe?

It's read-only. It never asks for your wallet, your seed phrase or a signature, and it never trades. The privacy policy lists exactly what it sends.

Does it work on my phone?

No. It runs in Chrome or Brave on a computer, inside Axiom and Terminal.

Does a GREEN trap check mean it's safe to buy?

No. It means no obvious traps were found. Most pump.fun coins still die, so size small and never trade money you can't lose.

Is a Pro alert a signal to sell?

No. It tells you what the launch wallets just did on-chain. What you do with that is up to you. Nothing here is financial advice.
