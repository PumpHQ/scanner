Pump HQ Scanner privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Pump HQ Scanner is a browser extension that adds labels, a coin panel and launch alerts to axiom.trade and pump.fun. This policy explains what it stores and what it sends.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, keywords, the dev wallets you track, your Scanner key and your recent alerts are saved in your browser's extension storage. They are not uploaded anywhere, except the Scanner key as described below.

What the extension sends

  • Coin addresses of the coins on your screen go to public market-data services (DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, pump.fun) to look up prices, candles and the dev's launch history.
  • A live feed of new pump.fun coins comes from PumpPortal's public feed, and Google's public daily trending searches come from trends.google.com. Nothing about you is sent to either.
  • If you add a Scanner key: your key and the address of a coin you check go to the Pump HQ scanner server, to confirm your subscription, run the trap check and fetch the list of really good devs. The server keeps a count of checks per key per day so it can enforce the daily limit, checks the key with Whop (where you bought it), and remembers each coin's result for a few minutes so repeat checks are fast. Your IP address is only used to rate-limit requests and is never saved.

What it never does

It never reads your wallet, your balances or your trades. It never signs transactions. It does not collect your browsing history, and no data is sold or shared for advertising.
